1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the Android mobile application Dukaan Hisab Pro ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), published under package name com.aistudio.dukaanhisab.kzpvqm.

We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of retail store owners, business proprietors, and their customers. This document details how information is handled when you use the Application.

2. Information We Handle

The Application is built to manage small-business accounting and customer credit ledgers. To perform these essential features, the user may enter the following data directly into the app:

  • Customer & Supplier Information: Names, contact phone numbers, optional business addresses, and notes.
  • Financial & Transaction Records: Credit given (उधार), payments received (जमा), sales bills, purchase invoices, cashbook receipts/payments, and expense entries.
  • Product & Inventory Data: Product names, cost prices, selling prices, and current stock quantities.
  • Store Profile Details: Shop name, shop type/category, owner name, contact number, and address displayed on invoices.

3. Local Storage & Zero External Server Sharing

All data entered into Dukaan Hisab Pro is stored exclusively in a sandboxed, on-device SQLite database (Room Database) on your mobile device.

  • We do NOT transmit your customer registers, debt balances, or invoices to our own or third-party marketing servers.
  • We do NOT track, sell, or rent your commercial transactions to advertisers, lenders, or data aggregators.
  • The Application operates fully offline without requiring an active internet connection for core accounting functions.

4. Device Permissions

In accordance with Google Play's Least-Privilege Principle, Dukaan Hisab Pro requests only standard permissions necessary for its intended functionality:

Permission Type Reason & Usage
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Install-time (Normal) Used solely for standard platform connectivity checks and optional user-initiated support actions. Core ledger features never depend on internet connectivity.

Note: The Application does NOT request broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). Any optional backup export or sharing is performed using the Android platform's zero-permission standard share intent framework.

5. Data Backup, Export & Portability

The Application gives you complete ownership and portability of your store records:

  • Offline JSON Export: You can create a complete encrypted/plain backup file of your ledger, products, and customer profiles at any time via the Backup screen.
  • Direct User Dispatch: The backup file is shared solely to destinations selected explicitly by you (e.g., your personal Google Drive, email, or messaging app) through Android's native system picker.

6. Data Deletion and Retention

Because your records reside locally on your mobile hardware:

  • In-App Clear All Entries: You can immediately erase all customer khata, invoices, expenses, cashbook transactions, and suppliers by navigating to Settings > Clear All Entries.
  • App Uninstall / Storage Clear: Clearing application storage via Android Settings > Apps > Dukaan Hisab Pro > Clear Data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all on-device database files and records.

7. Security Safeguards

We implement multiple layers of security to safeguard your commercial data:

  • Operating System Sandboxing: Android's application sandbox isolates Dukaan Hisab Pro's SQLite database, preventing other non-root applications from accessing your shop ledger.
  • Optional App PIN Lock: Users can activate an in-app 4-digit PIN lock under Settings to prevent unauthorized physical access to store books.

8. Third-Party Libraries & Analytics

Dukaan Hisab Pro does not employ third-party behavioral advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking networks, or intrusive tracking scripts.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is a business accounting utility designed for shopkeepers and retail merchants. It is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Continued use of the Application signifies your acceptance of the updated terms.
